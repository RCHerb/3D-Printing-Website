How It Works

1. Submit your project. Tell us what you need.

2. Receive your final quote. We review the model, material, print time, complexity, and any design work.

3. Approve and pay securely. Once you approve the quote, we send a secure Stripe invoice or payment link. Full payment is due before custom production begins.

4. We print your project. After payment is received, your approved project enters our production queue. No printing begins before payment.

Custom Project Payment Policy: We do not begin production until the final quote has been approved and payment has been received. If project requirements change after approval, we may provide a revised quote before continuing.
